Kaņepīte

Kaņepīte grows and produces local, ecologically clean hemp products in their native region of Latgale, Latvia. They reached out wanting to overhaul their brand and packaging while to to mark a strong presence online through a Shopify e-commerce store and future farmers market visits.

Brand design Packaging Web design

Client

Kaņepīte

Role

Designer and developer

Timeline

May – Jun 2025

Deliverables

Website, branding, print materials

Challenge

As the client had a limited budget and was running a small family business, it was essential to take into account the available resources and business capacity, while trying to make the projects deliverables usable and scalable in the case of a future business expansion.

Impact

Cohesive new branding across print and web, focused on the products advantages.

Branding

Context

I was approached to rewamp the logo and basic branding elements for Kaņepīte making it more inline with their business goals. The client wanted to focus on the informational aspects within the brand messaging, highlighting the health benefits of their products and focus on a more rustic design direction.

Key design decisions

The client wanted to retain elements of the old logo, namely, the latvian traditional St. James sign, which was then combined with a hemp leaf, resulting in a geometric logo accompanied by a sans serif font for a more natural feel.

Accompanied by an earthy green colour pallete, the brand new identity is meant to invoke a pastoral bliss of homegrown, ecologically clean produce grown and sold by an artisanal farmer- natural, nostalgic of the countryside and wholesome.

Packaging

Context

Packaging redesign consistent out three packaging types- bottles, jars and ziplock bags, with the future posibility of expansion.

Key design decisions

To keeep the packaging cohesive a unified design approach was taken, aplying the same dark colour theme and design priciples throughout the hemp oil and seeds design.

Website

Context

Following logo and and packaging handoff e-commerce store development commenced. The store was vital to furthering the business reach and establishing shipping possibilities.

Website was developed and hosted on Shopify based on a Shopify theme, that was later handed over for further coding adjustment. Initial design development was made in Figma through a sitemap and hi-fi wireframes. Store and shipping setup, product mock-ups, translation and user manual work was done by me.

Key design decisions

The e-commerce website mainly focuses on the health benefits and local, homegrown quality of the hemp products. Product cards were made made larger to focus on the products themselves.
