Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is procesed when you visit my website or contact me by email.

1. Who am I

The website is operated by Madara Grišāne, based in Latvia.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal data, you can contact me at: mgrisane@gmail.com

2. Personal data I receive

My website does not require you to create an account, complete a contact form, or provide personal information.

If you choose to contact me by clicking my email link, your email application will open and you can send a message to my Gmail address.

When I receive your email, I may process personal data contained in your message, such as:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • the contents of your message;
  • any other personal information you voluntarily include in your correspondence.

Please do not send sensitive or unnecessary personal information by email.

3. How and why your personal data is used

Information contained in email correspondence is used to:

  • respond to your inquiry;
  • communicate with you regarding your request;
  • provide information about my services;
  • take steps at your request before entering into a potential business relationship or contract, where applicable.

The legal basis for processing is generally Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where processing is necessary for our legitimate interest in responding to and managing communications. Where another legal basis applies, we will process the data on that basis.

4. Email service provider

My email address is provided through Gmail, a service operated by Google. Emails sent to me may therefore be processed and stored using Google's email infrastructure.

I use Gmail for receiving, storing, and managing email correspondence. Google's processing of personal data is also subject to Google's own privacy terms and policies.

5. How long your data is kept

I retain email correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain the relevant business communication, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

I may retain correspondence for longer where this is necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims or to comply with a legal obligation.

6. Sharing of personal data

I do not sell or rent your personal data.

Your correspondence may be accessible to me and to service providers used to operate my business, including my email service provider, Google.

I may also disclose personal data where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

7. Cookies, local storage and third-party services

This website does not use cookies for analytics, advertising, profiling, or other tracking purposes.

This website uses a consent-management tool that stores your consent preferences in your browser's local storage. This allows the website to remember your choices. The consent-management tool does not use cookies to track you.

This website may also load certain resources, such as JavaScript libraries and web fonts, from third-party content delivery networks. When these resources are requested, the relevant third-party provider may receive technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address, browser information, and the requested resource.

This website uses the following third-party services to provide website functionality:

  • jsDelivr — used to deliver the Alpine.js JavaScript library.
  • Google Fonts — used to provide the Inter and Lexend Mega fonts.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — used to provide Tailwind CSS functionality.

These services may process technical information transmitted by your browser when the relevant resources are requested. This website does not use these services for advertising or behavioral tracking.

Where applicable, information about third-party processing is also subject to the respective provider's privacy policies.

This website does not use Google Analytics, advertising trackers, behavioral profiling tools, or similar analytics technologies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • request access to your personal data;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of your personal data;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request data portability, where applicable.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

In Latvia, the supervisory authority is the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija).

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

There may be updates to this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be published on this page, together with the date of the latest update.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we process your personal data, please contact us at: mgrisane@gmail.com
